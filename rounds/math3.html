Sugar Jar Puzzle

You have three sugar jars with capacities of 12g, 8g, and 5g.

Initially, the 12g jar is full while the others are empty.

To pour sugar: first select the source jar, then select the destination jar.

Sugar will automatically pour until: the source jar becomes empty, or the destination jar becomes full.

Your goal is to make any jar contain exactly 6g.

Select a source jar, then select a destination jar.
